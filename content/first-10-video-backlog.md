## Selection order

Start with ATW-2026-001 and ATW-2026-002 only after their problem evidence and prototype tests are ready. They establish two distinct applied-AI capabilities: workflow automation and grounded knowledge assistance.

Do not choose a topic solely because it appears in this backlog. Use the opportunity scorecard in research/opportunity-system.md.

## Required record for every video

- Content ID and approved title.
- Audience and problem statement.
- Demand and competitor evidence.
- Workflow specification and alternatives considered.
- Test data description and privacy status.
- Test plan and results.
- Actual costs or clearly labelled estimates.
- Claim/source map.
- Approved script and storyboard.
- Asset manifest and rights status.
- Final export and QA record.
- Publishing package and post-publication review.

A video cannot claim savings, accuracy, conversion, or operational impact until the relevant measure has been collected with a defensible method.
