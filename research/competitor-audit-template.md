Coverage depth:
1. Mention only.
2. Concept explanation.
3. Tool walkthrough.
4. Partial workflow.
5. End-to-end implementation.
6. Tested workflow with constraints, evidence, and failure handling.

Do not infer that a channel is weak because a specific video is short or highly edited. Evaluate whether it answers the exact viewer question.

## Supply-gap statement

Write one precise and defensible sentence:

"Existing examples explain __________, but the evidence reviewed does not adequately show __________ for __________."

List inspected examples supporting this statement. Do not claim that no one covers something unless the review scope supports that conclusion.
