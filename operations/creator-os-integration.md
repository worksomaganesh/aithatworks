# AI That Works × Creator-OS Integration

## Purpose

Creator-OS is the shared operating infrastructure for independent creator brands. This repository is the AI That Works brand-specific operating layer.

**Reuse shared machinery; preserve independent editorial identity.**

Do not duplicate the complete Creator-OS system inside this repository. Do not move all AI That Works research or content records into the shared repository either. Use clear ownership boundaries.

## System responsibilities

### Creator-OS owns shared capabilities
- Common content lifecycle and structured handoff conventions.
- Shared research/evidence templates and quality gate patterns.
- Generic prompt/skill interfaces and reusable schemas.
- Common production planning, asset-manifest conventions, and QA patterns.
- Shared renderer and media-processing utilities where implemented.
- Cross-platform operational guidance that is genuinely common.

Canonical repository: https://github.com/worksomaganesh/Creator-OS

### AI That Works owns brand-specific decisions
- Positioning, audience, promise, and visual identity.
- Editorial boundaries and topic selection.
- Business problem research and competitor analysis.
- The AI That Works opportunity backlog and scores.
- Workflow specifications, test plans, and implementation evidence.
- Brand scripts, storyboards, prompts, and creative choices.
- Platform-specific publishing decisions and brand analytics.
- Services, products, offers, and customer research.

Canonical repository: https://github.com/worksomaganesh/aithatworks

## Content ID and data ownership

Use ATW-YYYY-NNN IDs for AI That Works items. Keep the content brief, source map, test evidence, implementation notes, production prompts, asset manifest, QA, and post-publication review traceable to that ID.

Creator-OS may define a generic schema or workflow state. The actual AI That Works content item remains in this repository or its approved media/operations storage.

## Shared workflow

Signal → research → opportunity evaluation → brand routing → ATW brief → workflow design → prototype/testing → evidence review → script/storyboard → production plan → media creation → editing → QA → human approval → platform publishing → analytics.

The AI That Works-specific research gate must establish a practical problem and audience outcome. A generic educational topic should not pass merely because the shared system can process it.

## Brand adapters

Creator-OS may contain a brand adapter for AI That Works, including:
- Brand ID: ATW.
- English language.
- Applied-AI implementation editorial role.
- Core question: what real problem can AI help solve and what does implementation require?
- Visual and presenter rules once formally approved.

A shared adapter stores configuration; it does not make the channel's content, research or voice shared with the other brands.

## Rules against duplication and coupling

- No mandatory cross-promotion.
- No duplicate content calendar or conflicting source of truth.
- No copying a single script into another brand.
- No dependence on another brand's audience, publishing schedule, or revenue.
- No use of another brand's logo, palette, avatar, or creative assets.
- No automatic routing based solely on a shared keyword; editorial purpose decides.
- Shared tools and subscriptions are allowed to reduce cost, but account credentials and private data must remain controlled.

## Suggested operating cadence

Keep shared infrastructure reviews separate from brand editorial reviews. A Creator-OS change should improve a reusable process; an AI That Works change should improve this brand's audience value, proof quality, production, or business operation.
